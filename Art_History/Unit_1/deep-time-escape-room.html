Field Assignment · Unit 1

Operation Deep Time

Five sealed layers stand between you and the Vault of Global Prehistory.

Your team has reached an excavation site holding eleven artifacts spanning 25,000 years. A rockslide sealed the trench into five layers, newest on top, oldest at the bottom — exactly how real archaeologists find deep time: you have to dig down through it, one layer at a time.

The complication: Dr. Voss and the Silvertrowel Society — a rival team with a reputation for "acquiring" sites before proper documentation — has been spotted at the ridge. Every time you clear a layer, expect them to make a move on your fragment before you can bank it.

Your mission: clear each layer in order — you can't skip ahead. Answer its questions correctly with your partner, then beat Voss's team to secure the fragment. Do this five times and the seal word will assemble on its own. Then face the Final Trial: rebuild the true chronological order before the vault opens.

Rules: talk out loud with your partner before you answer. Every attempt looks a little different, even for the pair next to you — the trench shifts each time.
Learning target: identify the title, culture/region, date range, and medium of each Unit 1 work, and apply unit vocabulary to analyze form, function, content, and context.
