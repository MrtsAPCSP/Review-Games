✒️ The Illuminator's Road

A Scribe's Pilgrimage Through the Making of a Gospel Book — Unit 3.3

Your abbot has chosen you for a sacred labor: to walk the road a manuscript itself must travel before it becomes holy. That road begins in the tannery and the old library, where hide becomes vellum and scribes still argue scroll versus codex. It winds into the hush of the scriptorium, where monks are among the only literate souls left in a fractured world — then past the chaos outside the monastery walls that makes this work so urgent. It leads to the illuminator's desk, where hammered gold and painted interlace share a common language, across the many traditions of medieval Europe, and at last to a finished Gospel book like the one made at Lindisfarne. Gather your companions, and begin.

Prologue · Rubric

📖 How the Pilgrimage Works

  • You'll travel the road a Gospel manuscript itself takes to be made, stopping at 6 waypoints before a final illumination at the scriptorium.
  • Each waypoint tests a different slice of Unit 3.3: parchment & the codex-vs-scroll debate, the unstable "Dark Ages" world that makes monastic learning so vital, monasteries & scholarship, metalwork motifs & the Merovingian fibula, geography & style across medieval Europe, and the finished Lindisfarne Gospels themselves.
  • Correct answers earn pigment drops 🎨 — these fill in your illuminated manuscript page at the final waypoint. Every playthrough randomizes the questions, so no two pilgrimages are the same.
  • Pilgrim Pair mode alternates who answers each question — talk it out before you choose!
  • Random events may strike along the road — a raider band, a lucky find, a wise hermit — so stay alert.
  • Finish the road to illuminate your manuscript page and see your full accuracy report. Then travel it again — the roads shift each time.
  • Want to review outside of class? Use Study by Candlelight anytime — no setup needed.
