The Lumen Society

A continuing mission through Ravenna & Constantinople — Byzantine Art, 500–1400s C.E.

Generations have passed since the Edict of Milan let your Society step into the light. Rome in the West now crumbles — but in the East, a dazzling New Rome rises on the shores of the Bosphorus: Constantinople. Your Society has endured, its keepers now sworn to guard something even more precious than secret symbols — the sacred images themselves.

Under Emperor Justinian, church and state become one, and gold-ground mosaics blaze inside soaring domes. But a danger is coming: some will soon say every icon is an idol, and mean to burn them all. Learn the treasures of Byzantium — and be ready to protect them when the fires come.
