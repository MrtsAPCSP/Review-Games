The Lumen Society

A secret mission through Catacombs & Cathedrals — Late Antiquity, 200–500 C.E.

Rome, c. 250 C.E. Christianity is illegal. To worship, you must go underground — literally. You are a member of a hidden community that gathers in the catacombs, marks its dead with secret symbols, and waits.

Then, in 313 C.E., everything changes. Learn the Faith's hidden signs, master the sacred architecture, and carry your community from darkness into the light of the first great basilicas.
