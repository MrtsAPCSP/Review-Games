You're piloting a deep-trench ROV by reading its dive-computer pseudocode before it executes. Trace each program in your head, then report what actually happens.

MOVE_FORWARD()
Moves 1 square in the direction the ROV is facing. Fails (collision/breach) if something's in the way!
ROTATE_LEFT() / ROTATE_RIGHT()
Turns 90°. Does NOT move the ROV.
CAN_MOVE(direction)
Returns true/false. Direction is forward, backward, left, or right — relative to the ROV's current facing.
PROCEDURE / REPEAT
Standard AP pseudocode blocks: IF/ELSE, REPEAT n TIMES, REPEAT UNTIL (condition), and reusable PROCEDUREs.
