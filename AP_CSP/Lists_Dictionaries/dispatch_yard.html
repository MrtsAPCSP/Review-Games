The Dispatch Yard

You're the new sorting clerk. Shipments come in fast, and every one has to land in the right place: the Conveyor if it's a Python list, or the Locker Wall if it's a Python dict. Mix them up and the yard jams.

Conveyor · list

Ordered slots, numbered from 0. Get to an item by position: belt[0]. Duplicates welcome.

Locker Wall · dict

Labeled lockers. Get to an item by its key: lockers["A1"]. Order isn't how you find things — the label is. Keys can't repeat.
