How the yard works

Conveyor = list. Things ride it in order. You reach an item by its position: belt[0] is the first thing on the belt, no matter what it is. The belt doesn't care if two boxes look identical — duplicates are fine.

Locker Wall = dict. Every locker has a label. You reach a locker by its label, not by where it physically sits: lockers["driver_id"]. Two lockers can never share a label — a repeated key just overwrites the old value.

Four kinds of shipments come down the chute:

  • Sort the Shipment — decide: does this belong on the conveyor or in the locker wall?
  • Predict the Jam — read code touching a list or dict, predict what happens.
  • Relabel — spot code that treats one structure like the other, and pick the fix.
  • Convert the Manifest — pick the code that correctly rebuilds the data in the other form.

Miss too many in one shift and the yard backs up — you'll retry the shift with a fresh set of shipments. No permanent game over.
